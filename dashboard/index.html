Reset lesson

Switching between CPU and Prometheus lessons replaces the autoscaler. Opening another lesson never changes the cluster until you confirm here.

  • Active traffic stops (manual load and one-click scenario lock).
  • Removes the old HPA app or ScaledObject app-prometheus / HPA keda-hpa-app-prometheus.
  • Restores app/edge manifests to baseline; app pods may restart.
  • Waits for exactly two Ready, non-terminating app pods and Ready endpoints.

Delete kind cluster?

This is separate from lesson reset. It deletes only the kind-scaling-lab cluster. Lesson switching never deletes the cluster.

Run in a terminal: make down

Clean up run?

  • Stops the run's traffic and deletes its namespace and all its resources (app, edge, scaler).
  • Other runs keep their namespaces, traffic routes, graphs, and evidence.
  • The run's evidence snapshot stays downloadable afterwards.
